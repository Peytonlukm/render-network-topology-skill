# Topology brief

Record supplied facts first. Reuse prior choices for a revision; do not require a fresh interview to make an already authorized change.

- Purpose, audience, canvas/destination, detail level and desired deliverables.
- Device IDs, roles, names, sites, groups, regions and deployment membership.
- Real edges and endpoints; physical/logical/management semantics; role, direction, status and uncertainty.
- Required paths, ring members, shared devices or links, actual bus junctions and pipe member IDs.
- Composition or combinations, fixed/locked positions, manual ports and routing constraints.
- Icon palette (default ocean), light/dark theme, label language, meaningful annotations and legend.
- Known gaps, assumptions requiring confirmation and layout conflicts requiring a decision.

Recommend a visual choice when the facts are sufficient. Ask concise questions only when missing information affects meaning or the requested result. Never infer connectivity from visual proximity.
